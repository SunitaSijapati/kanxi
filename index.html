01 / About

A student building the future of the web

Hello! I'm Sunita Sijapati — a curious, detail-obsessed creator currently studying Bachelor of Computer Application at Tribhuvan University, Kathmandu. I live at the intersection of technology and aesthetics.

My journey into tech began in high school when I redesigned my school's notice board as a simple website just for fun. That moment of "I built that" was addictive. Since then, I've been relentlessly learning, building, and refining my craft — from pixel-perfect Figma prototypes to fully deployed web applications.

I believe great digital products are born at the intersection of thoughtful engineering and intentional design. My philosophy is simple: every interaction should feel effortless, every pixel should have purpose.
